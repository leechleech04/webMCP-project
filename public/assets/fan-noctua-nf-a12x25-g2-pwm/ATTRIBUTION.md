## Disclosure of Production Method & Transformations
In accordance with Section 3(a)(1)(B) of the CC BY 4.0 International license:
1. **Source Photo Isolation:** The original photograph was cropped (pixel region [3200, 2100, 5400, 4300], 2200 × 2200 pixels) to isolate the centered brown/beige Noctua NF-A12x25 G2 fan.
2. **AI Draft Generation & Rejection:** An initial feedforward AI 3D draft was generated via TripoSR and evaluated by the independent verifier (Worker 04V). The AI draft was officially rejected (REJECT_REGENERATE) due to non-watertight topology and up to 1,644 disconnected islands, but retained unchanged in the evidence tree as historical reference.
3. **Deterministic Blender Reconstruction:** Deterministic manufacturer metadata is dimensional ground truth. The licensed photograph and rejected AI draft were visual references only. The rejected AI geometry supplies no compatibility or dimensional truth. A production 3D asset featuring seven individually watertight structural components was engineered in Blender 5.2.0:
   - Watertight 9-blade impeller with true 0.5mm tip clearance duct.
   - Exact 120 × 120 × 27 mm visual envelope with low-profile beveled anti-vibration corner pads (105 × 105 mm mounting holes).
   - Aerodynamic curved rear stator struts with central motor hub.
   - Restrained solid materials (solid Noctua beige frame, Sterrox brown blades, dark silicone brown corner pads, charcoal stator struts and hub; at most 4 materials/draw calls).
4. **Universal glTF Y-Up Coordinate System:** The glTF scene is natively Y-up with dimensions [120, 120, 27] mm (width X, height Y, depth Z), intake normal [0, 0, 1], exhaust normal [0, 0, -1], and center [0, 0, 0].
5. **LOD System:** Generates clean decimated LOD0 (18,878 tris), LOD1 (11,998 tris), and LOD2 (5,998 tris) with post-decimation clamping, zero floating islands, and 7 watertight components preserved across all LODs.
6. This 3D asset is an adaptation for visual rendering in SyncBuild. It is not endorsed by the photographer or Noctua.
